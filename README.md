# Penguin Slide and Bounce
or Penguin Bouncing on [itch.io](https://textralazy.itch.io/penguin-bouncing)


# What it's about
You have a penguin. You can Launch that penguin. Every time your penguin bounces you get a bounce back and you can spend your bounces for upgrades. Its basically a number go up game except the number goes up fast. 

# How to play and controls
Use your left mouse button and click and drag in the black box to launch your penguin(s) in whatever direction
